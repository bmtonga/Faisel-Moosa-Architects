About Us

Design to Communicate, Not to Decorate.

Faisel Moosa Architects provides high quality service, with a reputation for delivering exceptional design, production information and management of projects at all stages. Every project begins with the client’s goals and ends with a building that serves them.

Faisel Moosa Architects is a licensed professional architectural, planning and development services company based in Rhodes Park, Lusaka. Since July 2005 we have worked with individuals, builders, investors, developers and companies on residential and commercial projects across Zambia.

  • Licensed architectural, planning and development practice
  • Residential, commercial and refurbishment projects
  • Established in Lusaka in July 2005

We are the client’s advocate: crafting an envelope, achieving sustainability and creating value, while meeting the demands of the marketplace and staying on top of innovative design. Our services run from the first sketch to the finished building.

  • Architecture and interior design
  • Planning permission and development services
  • Construction-stage project management

Design does not follow a rigid formula or a cookie-cutter process, so we question everything and look for innovative ways to steer away from the norm. We work directly with the client, in collaborative efforts, to meet their needs and achieve their goals.

  • Collaborative design developed with the client
  • Exceptional design, production information and management
  • Involved at every stage, from concept to handover
