Effective 4 November 2026

Faisel Moosa Architects ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect through this website, why we collect it, how we keep it and the choices you have. It is written to reflect the Data Protection Act, No. 3 of 2021 of Zambia, which regulates how personal data is collected, processed and stored, and we aim to handle your information in line with it.

01Who we are

Faisel Moosa Architects is a licensed architectural, planning and development services company based at Plot 1146, No. 15 Lagos Road, Rhodes Park, Lusaka, Zambia. We are the data controller for personal information collected through this website.

You can contact us about this policy by email at creativityandflair@gmail.com, by telephone on +260 977 824 023, or by post at the address above.

02Information we collect

This website collects personal information in one place only: the enquiry form on the Contact page. When you send us an enquiry we receive:

  • your name;
  • your email address;
  • your telephone number, if you choose to give it;
  • the subject you select and the message you write; and
  • the date and time of the enquiry and the IP address of the device it was sent from, which our form handler records with the message to help us identify misuse.

Simply browsing the website does not require you to give us any personal information. The website does not use analytics, advertising or social-media tracking tools, and it does not create an account or profile for you. Your cookie preference is kept in your browser only. See our Cookie Policy for details of the one third-party service we embed.

03How we use your information

We use the information from an enquiry to:

  • read and reply to your enquiry;
  • discuss a possible project with you and, if you engage us, carry out that work; and
  • keep a record of our correspondence.

The lawful basis for this processing is that you have asked us to respond to you and, where a project follows, that the processing is necessary to perform our agreement with you. We do not use enquiry details for marketing and we do not add you to a mailing list.

04How we store and protect it

Your enquiry is sent to us by email and kept in our email system and, where a project follows, in our project records. We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect it: the website is served over HTTPS, the form rejects malformed submissions and automated spam, and access to our email and records is limited to the people who need it to respond to you.

We keep enquiry correspondence for as long as needed to deal with your enquiry and any project that follows, and afterwards for the period required by our professional and record-keeping obligations.

05Sharing your information

We do not sell personal information and we do not share it with third parties for their own purposes. We share it only:

  • with service providers who help us run the website and our email, who act on our instructions (our web host and email provider);
  • with consultants and contractors on your project where you have engaged us and the sharing is necessary to carry out the work; and
  • where the law requires it or to protect our legal rights.

If any of these providers store information outside Zambia, we take steps to ensure it is protected to a standard comparable to Zambian law, as the Data Protection Act requires for cross-border transfers.

06Your rights

Under the Data Protection Act, 2021 you have the right to:

  • ask for access to the personal information we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct information that is inaccurate or incomplete;
  • ask us to delete information we no longer need;
  • object to, or ask us to restrict, processing in certain circumstances;
  • withdraw consent where processing is based on consent; and
  • lodge a complaint with the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner of Zambia if you believe we have handled your information unlawfully.

To exercise any of these rights, email creativityandflair@gmail.com. We will respond within the time the law allows and may ask you to confirm your identity first.

07Children

This website is intended for adults and businesses. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has sent us information, contact us and we will delete it.

08Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of the page shows when it was last changed, and the current version is always the one published here. This version is effective from 4 November 2026.
