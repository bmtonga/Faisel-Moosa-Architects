Who We Are

Design to Communicate, Not to Decorate.

Faisel Moosa Architects is a licensed professional architectural, planning and development services company based in Rhodes Park, Lusaka. Founded in July 2005 by Faisel Moosa, the practice works with individuals, builders, investors, developers and companies on residential, commercial and refurbishment projects across Zambia.

We see ourselves as the client’s advocate: crafting an envelope, achieving sustainability and creating value, while meeting the demands of the marketplace and staying on top of innovative design.

Our Philosophy

Architecture With Intention

A building has to do more than look right. It has to work for the people who use it, sit comfortably in its place and go on performing long after the opening day. So we start every project with the brief, the site and the climate, and let the form grow out of those.

Design does not follow a rigid formula or a cookie-cutter process. We question everything, look for innovative ways to steer away from the norm, and test each idea against the practical questions of budget, construction and maintenance before it reaches the drawing.

The result we aim for is architecture that communicates: spaces whose purpose is clear, whose materials are honest and whose value to the client outlasts fashion.

Our Commitment to the Future

Designing Beyond Today

Lusaka’s strong sun, dry winters and heavy rains shape every building we draw. Orientation, roof overhangs, shaded openings and cross-ventilation are decided in the first sketches, so that comfort comes from the design before it comes from equipment.

We favour durable materials that age well and can be sourced and maintained locally, plan for drainage and water, and lay out buildings so they can adapt as their owners’ needs change.

These are working principles rather than certifications: the measure of success is a building that is still comfortable, economical and useful twenty years after it is handed over.
