About Us

Faisel Moosa Architects

Faisel Moosa Architects was founded in July 2005 by Faisel Moosa. We are a licensed professional architectural, planning and development services company that works with individuals, builders, investors, developers and companies on both residential and commercial projects.

The Client’s Advocate

Crafting an envelope, achieving sustainability and creating value, while meeting the demands of the marketplace and staying on top of innovative design.

Design, Production & Management

A reputation for delivering exceptional design, production information and management of projects at all stages.

Architecture Design

Design does not follow a rigid formula or a cookie-cutter process. We question everything, look for innovative ways to steer away from the norm, and work directly with the client to meet their needs and achieve their goals.

Planning & Development

Licensed professional architectural, planning and development services, including the preparation and management of planning permission applications.

Project Management

During construction we represent the owner: answering the contractor’s questions, conducting site observation visits, monitoring progress, preparing change orders and reviewing construction drawings.

Interior Design

Interior design completes the project and defines its image. Our design team collaborates with the client on the selection of colours, materials, textures and furniture, fixtures and equipment (FF&E).
