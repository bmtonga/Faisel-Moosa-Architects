Your Representative on Site

During the construction period we make ourselves available to the contractor to answer any questions and address any concerns they may have. We are involved throughout construction to represent the owner: conducting site observation visits, monitoring progress, preparing any required change orders, reviewing construction drawings and managing planning permission applications.

Site observation

Regular visits to check that the work follows the drawings and specification, with written reports after each one.

Progress and change

Programme and cost monitored against the contract; change orders prepared, priced and agreed before work proceeds.

From Contractor Appointment to Handover

We help the client assemble tender documents, compare the contractors' prices and appoint the contractor on a clear contract. Once on site, queries are answered promptly so the programme is not held up waiting for decisions.

As the building nears completion we inspect the work, list the items to be corrected and certify completion only when they are done, then hand over the as-built drawings and operating information.

  • Tender documents and contractor appointment
  • Site observation visits and reports
  • Programme, cost and change-order control
  • Inspections, defects lists and handover

What You Receive

A building delivered as designed, a documented record of decisions made on site, and a single point of contact who answers for the project throughout construction.

The owner's interests first

We are appointed by you, not the contractor, and our reports and certificates reflect that.

Continuity from the drawing board

The architects who designed the building are the ones checking it on site.
